# Persona questionnaire

Send as one message. Adjust the wording to the person's language and business.

## Your best customers

1. Think of your three to five best customers. Who are they (role, type of company or life situation)?
2. What do they do day to day, and where does your offer fit in?
3. What made them choose you? What almost stopped them?
4. What do they value most (for example price, speed, expertise, personal contact, security)?
5. What do they look for first on your website? What do they ask you that the website should answer?
6. Who else is involved when they decide, and how long does it take?
7. How do they usually find you?
8. Is there a type of customer you do not want?

## The website

9. What should a visitor do on the website (call, book, buy, request a demo, sign up)?
10. How will we know in six months that the new website works better than the old one?

## Validation call (optional, 15 minutes per persona)

Use when the client can talk to one real person per persona. Show nothing; ask, listen, take notes.

1. Tell me about the last time you looked for a solution like this. What triggered it?
2. Where did you look, and what did you want to find out first?
3. What made you trust a provider, and what made you leave a website?
4. Which words would you type into a search or an AI assistant?
5. Who else had a say, and what did they need to see?

Afterwards: update the persona, remove the `(assumption)` marks that were confirmed, correct the rest and log the call in `DECISIONS.md`.
